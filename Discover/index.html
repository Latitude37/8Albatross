Banksia + Moonah

Two homes in one of the Mornington Peninsula’s quieter pockets.

Set on approximately 1,000sqm each, Banksia and Moonah are taking shape on Albatross Avenue, surrounded by established trees and open farmland.

This is a more peaceful side of the Mornington Peninsula. Close to the beach, village shops, cafés and boutiques, but away from the busier holiday areas further south. And with Melbourne’s CBD just an hour away, it offers a rare balance of space, peace and city connection.

The residences

Banksia & Moonah

Two distinct homes. Each with its own character.

Banksia and Moonah take a restrained approach to luxury, shaped through natural materials, generous proportions and considered interiors.

There is a sense of ease to both homes. Space to retreat, room to gather, and a level of privacy that suits the way people want to live on the Peninsula.

The result is quiet luxury in a way that feels right for Mount Eliza. Connected to the coast, close to Melbourne, and removed from the busier pace further south.

Completion is anticipated in 2027.

8 Albatross Avenue

Banksia

Banksia is the brighter of the two homes, with light neutrals, timber and expansive glazing creating a relaxed, natural character.

The main floor brings the kitchen, dining, living spaces and bedrooms together across one level, opening directly to the alfresco, pool and landscaped garden.

Below, the garage, study, workshop and cellar are arranged around the entry, with lift access to the main floor.

Banksia feels open and relaxed, with everyday living centred around light, landscape and a strong indoor-outdoor connection.

8A Albatross Avenue

Moonah

Moonah takes a darker material direction, softened by warm timber, natural interior finishes and layered landscaping.

Kitchen, dining, living spaces and bedrooms are brought together across the main floor, with the living areas opening directly to the alfresco, pool and landscaped backyard.

Garage, study and workshop sit on the level below, with lift access to the main floor.

The deeper palette gives Moonah its own identity, while the connection between living, landscape and pool keeps the home relaxed and suited to Peninsula life.
